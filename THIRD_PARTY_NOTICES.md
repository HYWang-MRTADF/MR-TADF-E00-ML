# Third-party notices

## Deep4Chem optical-properties database

The Deep4Chem-derived records originate from Joung, J. F.; Han, M.; Jeong, M.;
Park, S. "Experimental database of optical properties of organic compounds."
*Scientific Data* **7**, 295 (2020), DOI `10.1038/s41597-020-00634-8`.

The dataset source used by the project is "DB for chromophore," Figshare record
12045567, version 2, DOI `10.6084/m9.figshare.12045567.v2`. Local project license
records identify it as CC BY 4.0. The included records are changed derivatives:
toluene filtering, canonicalization, E0,0 recalculation, structure-level
aggregation, and cross-domain overlap reconciliation were applied.

## MR-TADF literature compilation

The MR-TADF records are an author-curated factual compilation with row-level
references retained in `data/E00_UNIFIED_1396.csv`. No article or Supporting
Information PDF is included.
